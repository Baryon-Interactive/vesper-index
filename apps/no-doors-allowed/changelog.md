## 0.3.4 (2026-11-19)

- The first job now ends with a real drive: after loading the painting, the crew gets in the van and drives to the training garage. There are no police on the way, and R puts the van back on the road if you get stuck.
- The first job shows the key to press at every step, including getting in and out of the van.
- Voice chat no longer starts with your microphone open. Hold Caps Lock to talk, or pick Toggle or Always on in the settings. The browser only asks for microphone access the first time you press the key, and the crew panel shows who is speaking.
- Rain is quieter by default and has its own volume slider in the settings.
- Everyone in an online crew needs 0.3.4.

## 0.3.3 (2026-11-19)

- You can now see what your crewmates are holding and doing: tools, guns, throws and carried loot show in their hands, and two players lifting a safe each hold one side.
- Rain sounds steady instead of cutting out every few seconds. Heavy and light rain look different, wind slants it, roofs and vans keep it off, and puddles ripple and splash.
- Walls and floors no longer shimmer with wavy patterns at a distance or under neon light.
- In the first job, hammering the boards breaks them off row by row, and everyone in the crew sees the hole. Tools now tell you when a material is too hard for them.
- Parked crew vans take a beating before they break: a hammer dents them first and tears the panel after about a dozen hits.
- Fixed the van starting stuck against a wall in the warehouse back alley.
- The Windows build also gets the fixes from 0.3.1 and 0.3.2.
- Everyone in an online crew needs 0.3.3; older versions no longer join the same rooms.

## 0.3.2 (2026-11-19)

- Online co-op in the browser now falls back to a relay server when two players cannot connect directly, so crews behind strict home or office networks can still play together.
- Fixed errors while joining a crew before the first job loads, and a glitch in the studio intro.

## 0.3.1 (2026-11-19)

- Online co-op now works in the browser version: Play online connects to the public lobby, where you can open a room, browse open rooms or join by room code.

## 0.3.0 (2026-11-19)

- A guided first job walks new crews through the basics, and the van's tabs open up over the first four jobs.
- Crew vehicles now load through the rear doors. Carry loot to the back of the van; the side door stays shut.
- Pick a specialty: structural engineer, demolition expert, hauler or driver.
- Plan ahead in the new PREP tab: book favours and move timed events before the job.
- New heavy gear: a folding bridge plank, a cart, mobile cover, a rappel kit, a hydraulic jack, a cargo hoist and portable scaffolding. Plus a bore gun, a freight zipline and a sewer escape route out of the bank.
- Clients offer their own jobs in a new CLIENTS tab, and a police radio reports what the crew is doing during the chase.
- Street reputation, medals and a trophy shelf in the garage, a crew report after each job, and a collection of van looks to unlock.
- Chase tuning: adjustable difficulty, better braking, an autopilot for testing and a steadier camera.
- Music now shifts between sneaking, alarm and chase, and ducks while someone speaks. A separate music volume is in the settings.
- Health slowly comes back after a few seconds out of combat, the planning map can clear all marks, and a structural readout warns who caused a collapse.
- Online crews can rejoin within 60 seconds after a disconnect.
- The Windows build is renamed No Doors Allowed, opens with a new Baryon Interactive intro, and is compiled ahead of time for faster, harder-to-tamper code. Older Windows saves are copied over on first launch.

## 0.2.0 (2026-11-18)

- The starter van and the crew vehicles are rebuilt in finer 5 cm voxels, with new paints, liveries, roof racks, light bars and other exterior parts. The garage has sub-tabs and tooltips.
- Driving feels heavier and easier to read: steering ramps up, grip depends on the van's weight, the van scrapes along walls instead of bouncing off them, the turning radius is wider, and the body and camera lean into corners.
- Five performance upgrades in a new garage tab: engine, tyres, armour, bumper and nitro (hold Shift in a chase).
- Gamepad input for driving in the getaway chase.

## Every wall is a door

No Doors Allowed is a heist game built from small voxels. Walls, floors and ceilings are made of 12.5 cm blocks, and all of them can be cut, smashed or blown apart. Pick a building, decide where you want to go in, and make your own entrance.

## Plan, breach, carry, escape

- **Plan from the van.** Every job starts with a contract: a generated building, a target and a few twists, such as a vault upstairs, a sealed back alley or sensors on every ground-floor wall. Study the layout before you commit.
- **Breach your way.** Saw through plaster, sledge through brick, burn through steel with thermite or blow it with charges. Pre-cut a wall quietly and break it later, ram the warehouse with the van, or rent the shop next door and dig in from below.
- **Mind the structure.** Buildings carry their own weight. Take out the wrong wall and the floor above comes down, on the loot or on you. Brace it with steel struts when you need to.
- **Loot has weight.** Jewellery trays, watches, paintings, safes and heavy cargo all take up space and have to be carried out by hand. Fragile pieces lose value when they are dropped or caught in a blast.
- **The alarm always goes off.** The quiet phase is for setting up. When the alarm sounds, steel shutters drop, police arrive in waves and breachers blow their own holes in the walls.
- **Lose them in the van.** Drive through a generated city and reach a garage without being seen. Crash too hard and the cargo falls out the back.

## Run a crew

Between jobs you pay back the fixer, choose who to sell to, buy gear and struts, and repaint the van. A daily building gives everyone the same job each day, and the weekly 3-job strings three buildings together with no restock in between.

## Early build

This is an in-development build, playable solo in your browser or on Windows. Co-op for up to four players exists in the game, but its online lobby is not running on Vesper yet. Saves stay in your browser or on your PC.
